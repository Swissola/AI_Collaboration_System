# Health & Fitness Example - Evolution Timeline

Shows how a fitness project evolves from Day 1 through Month 2 using friction-driven development.

---

## Day 1: Initial Setup (30 minutes)

### Layer 1: Personal Preferences (Account-wide)
- Always explain reasoning before giving recommendations
- Challenge my assumptions when they might lead to poor outcomes
- Prioritise sustainable approaches over quick fixes

### Layer 2: Project Context (Five Essentials)

**Context:**
Focused on building sustainable fitness habits after previous intense programmes failed. Beginner level for most movements but understand training principles. Working around variable schedule with home equipment only.

**Constraints:**
- Time: 4-5 hours/week, schedule varies weekly
- Equipment: Dumbbells 5-25kg, resistance bands, pull-up bar (home only)
- Budget: £50/month maximum
- Previous 6-day programmes unsustainable, occasional lower back sensitivity

**Goals:**
- Train consistently 4x/week for 3+ months
- Build foundational strength progressively without injury
- Maintain high energy throughout workday

**Preferences:**
- Evidence-based recommendations over fitness industry hype
- Want to understand WHY things work, not just WHAT to do
- Challenge unrealistic expectations constructively

**Red Flags:**
- Don't suggest gym-based programmes (no access)
- Don't recommend 5-6 day/week routines (tried, can't sustain)
- Don't assume unlimited time or that fitness is top priority

### Layer 3: Project Memory
Empty - will build through friction

---

## Week 1: First Friction Patterns (15 minutes)

**What caused friction:**
- Equipment assumptions: Suggested barbell squats, cable exercises, leg press (3x) → AI assumes gym despite Layer 2 stating home equipment
- Workout complexity: 90-min programmes, 6-exercise circuits, elaborate warm-ups (3x) → AI suggests more than time allows

**Layer 3 added:**

**Memory #1 - Equipment check:**
When suggesting exercises → Always verify available equipment first → Don't assume gym access → Prevents suggesting impossible workouts

**Memory #2 - Workout time reality:**
When designing sessions → Keep to 45-60 min maximum → Don't suggest 90+ min elaborate programmes → Time constraint is real

---

## Month 2: Expanded System (30 minutes additional)

### Layer 2 Expansions Added

**Operating Modes (added Week 4):**
- Mode 1: Planning & Strategy - comprehensive, explore alternatives, 4-12 week timelines
- Mode 2: Session Execution - concise, specific answers, minimal explanation

**Decision Framework (added Week 6):**
- Priority 1: Sustainability & Consistency
- Priority 2: Safety & Injury Prevention
- Priority 3: Progressive Overload
- Priority 4: Enjoyment

### Layer 3 Additional Memory

**Memory #3 (Week 4) - Progressive complexity:**
When I'm struggling with movement → Suggest ONE regression to work on → Don't provide 5 alternatives at once → Prevents analysis paralysis

**Memory #4 (Week 5) - Recovery context:**
When suggesting frequency/volume → Ask about current recovery state first → Don't assume optimal conditions → Life stress affects training capacity

**Memory #5 (Week 7) - Form cues:**
When explaining technique → Provide 2-3 key cues focusing on what to FEEL → Don't list 10 technical points → Can't think about 10 things whilst lifting

---

## Evolution Summary

**Day 1:** Layer 1 (3 items) + Layer 2 (5 essentials) + Layer 3 (empty)

**Week 1:** Added 2 memory instructions from friction

**Month 2:** Layer 2 expanded (+2 sections), Layer 3 matured (5 total instructions)

**Total time:** 30 min setup → 15 min Week 1 → 30 min Month 2 = 75 min over 8 weeks

**Key insight:** Started minimal, evolved through friction, not upfront planning.
